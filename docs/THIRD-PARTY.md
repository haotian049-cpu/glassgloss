The technical dictionaries are bilingual, text-only adaptations of the linked glossaries. They were converted to native macOS Dictionary format with lookup aliases and, where useful, short Chinese summaries. Sources, license text, and the nature of the modification are recorded in each bundle and entry. No endorsement is implied.

Apple language assets are installed separately through macOS. No commercial dictionary data is bundled.
